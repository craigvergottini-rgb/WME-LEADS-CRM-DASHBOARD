Technical Knowledge Base & South Africa Market Guide

Balwin Properties estate specs, chrome washplants, hospital networks, surface water intake, and decision-maker directories.

1. Balwin Properties & Eco-Estate Greywater Systems

Overview: Balwin Properties is SA's leading eco-friendly residential developer. Operates dedicated divisions: Balwin Green Living (Renewables & Wastewater) and Balwin Infrastructure.

Key Flagship Estates: Mooikloof Smart City (Tshwane), The Reid (Linbro Park), Greenlee, Greencreek, Greenpark, The Blyde (Pretoria East), Polofields (Waterfall).

Water Treatment Applications:

  • Greywater Harvesting: Reclaiming shower/basin discharge for estate landscape irrigation and flushing lifestyle center toilets.
  • Wastewater Recycling & MBR: Onsite Membrane Bioreactor (MBR) treatment plants to achieve EDGE Green & 6-Star Green Star rating compliance.

Target Decision-Makers: Steve Brookes (CEO), Head of Balwin Infrastructure, Balwin Green Living Asset Manager, Precinct Engineering Lead.

2. South African Chrome Washplant Operators & Processing

Key Operators & Producers: Tharisa Minerals, Samancor Chrome, Glencore Ferrochrome, Hernic Ferrochrome, Jubilee Metals Group (tailings retreatment), ASA Metals, Afarak Holdings.

Geographic Concentration: Bushveld Igneous Complex (Eastern Limb: Steelpoort, Burgersfort, Tubatse; Western Limb: Rustenburg, Brits, Thabazimbi).

Process Water Treatment Applications:

  • Thickener Overflow & Clarification: High-rate clarification and flocculant dosing for spiral and DMS washplant circuits.
  • Slimes Dam Decant & Ultrafine Fines: Filtering ultrafine chromite suspended solids for closed-loop water recovery.

Target Decision-Makers: General Manager (GM), Plant Metallurgist, Resident Engineer (GCC), Environmental Manager.

3. Ground / Surface Water Treatment Systems

Target Facilities: River abstraction plants, dam intake installations, agricultural estates, rural municipal utilities, remote lodges.

Water Treatment Applications:

  • Coagulation & Flocculation: Dosing poly-aluminum chloride (PAC) for colloidal particle settling.
  • Dissolved Air Flotation (DAF) & Clarification: Removing high turbidity, organic algae blooms, and suspended silt.
  • Ultrafiltration (UF) & Media Filtration: Multi-barrier particulate and pathogen removal delivering SANS 241 potability.

Target Decision-Makers: Water Resources Engineer, Infrastructure Operations Manager, Agricultural Director, Municipal Water Engineer.

4. Greywater & Medical Water (Hospital Networks)

Target Networks in SA: Netcare, Mediclinic, Life Healthcare, Busamed, NHLS, Regional Public Hospitals.

Water Treatment Applications:

  • Hemodialysis Pure Water: High-purity RO systems complying with SANS 241 & ISO 13959 medical water specs.
  • Greywater Reuse: Reusing basin water for sanitation and estate grounds.
  • Water Security Backup: Borehole and greywater purification to avoid operational shutdown during municipal outages.

Target Decision-Makers: Group Technical Engineer, Clinical Engineering Manager, Hospital GM, Infection Control Lead.

SA Regulatory Framework: SANS 241 & DWS Licensing

SANS 241 Drinking Standard: Specifies limits for drinking water (Microbiological: 0 E. coli; Chemical: Nitrate < 11 mg/L, Iron < 0.3 mg/L, TDS < 1200 mg/L).

DWS Water Use License (WULA): Required under National Water Act for commercial borehole abstraction, surface water intake, and industrial discharge into natural watercourses.
