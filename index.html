Act 36 of 1947 & SANS Compliance Matrix

Regulatory standards, pest control formulation frameworks (L-numbers), and SANS water quality benchmarks.

Act 36 of 1947 Framework

  • Product Registration (L-Numbers): Official registration requirement for stock remedies and agricultural/pest formulations under South African law.
  • Boric Acid Gel Baits: Specific formulation standards for pest management solutions in commercial/industrial food and water handling sites.
  • Active Ingredient Compliance: Strict label registration, active concentrations, and MSDS safety sheet management.

SANS 10241 Water Quality Matrix

  • Microbiological Safety: Zero E. coli / 100 ml, Legionella pneumophila control under 100 CFU/L in HVAC cooling towers.
  • Physical & Chemical Parameters: pH limits (6.5 – 9.5), Turbidity (< 1 NTU), Electrical Conductivity (< 170 mS/m).
  • Heavy Metals & Corrosion: Iron (< 0.3 mg/L), Manganese (< 0.1 mg/L), scale inhibition metering.
