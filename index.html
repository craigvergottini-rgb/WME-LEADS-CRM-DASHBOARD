Technical Knowledge Base & South Africa Market Guide

Balwin Properties estate specs, chrome washplants, hospital networks, surface water intake, and key decision-makers.

1. Balwin Properties & Eco-Estate Greywater Systems

Overview: Leading eco-friendly residential developer operating Balwin Green Living and Balwin Infrastructure.

Flagship Estates: Mooikloof Smart City, The Reid, Greenlee, Greencreek, Greenpark, The Blyde, Polofields.

Treatment Applications: Greywater harvesting for irrigation and toilet flushing; onsite MBR wastewater treatment.

Key Decision-Makers: Steve Brookes (CEO), Head of Balwin Infrastructure, Balwin Green Living Asset Manager.

2. South African Chrome Washplant Operators & Processing

Key Operators: Tharisa Minerals, Samancor Chrome, Glencore Ferrochrome, Hernic Ferrochrome, Jubilee Metals Group.

Concentration: Bushveld Igneous Complex (Steelpoort, Burgersfort, Tubatse, Rustenburg, Brits).

Treatment Applications: Thickener overflow clarification, flocculant dosing, slimes dam decant water recovery.

Key Decision-Makers: General Manager (GM), Plant Metallurgist, Resident Engineer (GCC).

3. Ground / Surface Water Treatment Systems

Target Facilities: River abstraction plants, dam intakes, agricultural estates, rural municipal utilities.

Treatment Applications: Coagulation (PAC dosing), Dissolved Air Flotation (DAF), Ultrafiltration (UF) delivering SANS 241 potability.

Key Decision-Makers: Water Resources Engineer, Infrastructure Operations Manager, Agricultural Director.

4. Greywater & Medical Water (Hospital Networks)

Target Networks: Netcare, Mediclinic, Life Healthcare, Busamed, NHLS.

Treatment Applications: High-purity hemodialysis RO (SANS 241 / ISO 13959), greywater reuse for sanitation, backup purification.

Key Decision-Makers: Group Technical Engineer, Clinical Engineering Manager, Hospital GM.

5. Key Target Decision-Makers Matrix

Developers: Head of Infrastructure, Green Living Manager, Precinct Operations Director.

Chrome & Mining: General Manager, Plant Metallurgist, Resident Engineer (GCC).

Hospitals: Group Technical Engineer, Clinical Engineering Manager, Infection Control Lead.

6. SA Regulatory Framework: SANS 241 & DWS Licensing

SANS 241 Standard: Zero E. coli / 100ml, Nitrate < 11 mg/L, Iron < 0.3 mg/L, TDS < 1200 mg/L.

DWS WULA License: Required under National Water Act for commercial borehole abstraction and industrial discharge.
